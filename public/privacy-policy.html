Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 7, 2026

At Astro Eleven, accessible from astroeleven.com and our mobile application, your privacy is one of our main priorities. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by Astro Eleven and how we use, protect, and handle your data.

🔒 Privacy Commitment: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal birth details, consultation logs, or conversation recordings to third-party advertisers. All data is processed solely for providing astrology calculations and direct user consultations.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when registering on the application or requesting a consultation:

  • Personal Identifiers: Name, mobile phone number, email address, gender, and preferred language.
  • Birth Details: Date of birth, exact time of birth, and place of birth (city, state, country) necessary for generating accurate Vedic Rasi charts, KP chart significations, and horoscope predictions.
  • Consultation Data: Call durations, chat history, and ratings/feedback provided for astrologer sessions.
  • Payment & Wallet Data: Transaction ID, recharge amount, and payment status. We do not store sensitive payment details like credit card numbers or UPI PINs; all payments are processed directly by RBI-authorized payment gateways (e.g., PhonePe).

2. Sensitive Permissions (Mobile Application)

Our Android application requests permissions only when required for active features:

  • Camera & Microphone: Used exclusively during live 1-on-1 audio and video consultation calls with astrologers using encrypted WebRTC technology.
  • Notifications: To alert you when an astrologer accepts your call request or sends a message.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate precise horoscope calculations and astronomical chart positions.
  • To facilitate real-time audio, video, and chat communication with certified astrologers.
  • To credit and debit your Astro Eleven wallet balance accurately based on per-minute consultation rates.
  • To notify you about special offers, planetary transit alerts, and account updates.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your account information as long as your account remains active. You have the right to request full account and data deletion at any time. You can visit our Account Deletion Page to initiate account removal.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding our Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@astroeleven.com.
