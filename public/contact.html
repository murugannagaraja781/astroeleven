Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add funds to my Astro Eleven Wallet?
Click on your Profile in the app or website, choose Wallet Recharges, select an amount, and complete payment securely via UPI, Credit/Debit Card, or Netbanking.
What happens if my call gets disconnected?
If a call drops due to technical reasons, our system automatically refunds unused minutes back to your Astro Eleven wallet within minutes.
How can an Astrologer register to join Astro Eleven?
Astrologers can apply directly via our website by submitting their credentials or emailing us at support@astroeleven.com for our team verification process.
